Sigma Premier Hotel In Ohio

A charming hotel experience in Ohio, with overwater chalets and sky tower units offering stunning views of the Ohio landscape.

Your search for serenity and peace comes to a fulfilling end at Sigma Premier Hotel. Once you step into this charming paradise, featuring stunning sky-high towers and inviting water chalets,
you'll be fully engulfed by an immense sense of belonging and be at one with nature.

All 392 chalets and tower rooms are designed for the ultimate holiday experience, combining charm, elegance, and comfort within its eclectic design, with thoughtful touches of indulgence everywhere you look.
Each unit is adorned with exquisite stone and hardwood flooring and comes with a private balcony overlooking the magnificent surrounding views.
Perched over the tranquil waters of Ohio's lakes, the water chalets are also fitted with see-through glass floor panels, so guests can enjoy a calming view of the water beneath at any time.
You can spend your time relishing the surrounding natural bliss, or simply indulge in the comfort of your room as well as all the wonderful facilities and amenities that the hotel has to offer.
In addition to that, you can also rely on the staff of the hotel to ensure that your stay here is always a pleasant one.
Honed by more than a decade's worth of experience, the hotel offers nothing but the best services and local hospitality to each and every guest.

Sigma Premier Hotel is situated near the heart of Ohio, just a few kilometers from major attractions.
It is easily accessible via the main highways and is about an hour's drive from Cleveland and about 30 minutes' drive from the Akron-Canton Airport.

Experience the allure of Sigma Premier Hotel, where water chalets, Ohio's natural charm, and a delightful escape await you.
Book your stay at this exquisite Ohio hotel today, and immerse yourself in a world of tranquility and natural beauty.
