Rooms & Chalets

Recharge, Reconnect in Comfort & Harmony

Accommodation at Sigma Premier Hotel comprises the modestly proportioned Deluxe Water Chalets and Executive Water Chalets, to the spacious Premium Water Chalets,
each delivering a one-of-a-kind immersive experience to guests who seek a relaxing over-the-water living experience. For a taste of sky-high living, the Premium Tower Sea view is recommended
with its unrestricted views of the resort’s picturesque vicinity stretching far into the sea, in perfectly harmonious unity with its natural setting.

All the 392 water chalets and tower rooms are furnished with king and queen beds, or king and day beds, within a cosy environment with concealed lightings.
Guests also enjoy the luxury of a private balcony for added privacy and convenience, also favoured for fishing.
The water chalets also come with open-concept bathrooms for a more unique experience.

Escape to a waterfront paradise at Sigma Premier Hotel, your premier choice for a remarkable water chalet resort in Ohio, United States.
Embrace the breathtaking views, feel the gentle sea breeze on your skin, and surrender to the tranquility of your surroundings.
