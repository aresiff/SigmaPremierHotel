Premium Tower Room

Located on the 5th to the 11th floor of the main tower block, these rooms offer sky-high living where guests can enjoy
unobstructed views of the sea and the rest of the quaint resort. Each of the 605 square feet units is well-equipped with
everything you need for a comfortable getaway, including a lavish bathtub where you can enjoy delightful bubble baths,
and 1 king size and 1 queen size bed that can fit four people cozily. The strategic layout of the rooms’ interior allows you
to enjoy a stunning view of the sea while you laze in the comfort of the beds. What better way to start your day than to
wake up to a gorgeous sea view?

Deluxe Water Chalet

Each of the 418 square feet Deluxe Water Chalets comes with 1 king size bed and 1 day bed. Perched over the sea,
the chalets either come with full sea views, or partial sea views along with the lovely surrounding resort compound.

Executive Water Chalet

Each of the 510 square feet Executive Water Chalets is luxuriously airy and furnished with 1 king size bed and 1 day bed.
To add a touch of elegance, the king size bed comes with a lustrous canopy draped across the top. Every unit comes with
a separate shower and bathtub for added indulgence and convenience. There is also a private balcony that is equipped
with comfortable deckchairs, where you can lie back and relax with a cuppa while enjoying the splendid sea view.

Premium Water Chalet

Each Premium Water Chalet comes with 1 king size bed and 1 queen size bed which can fit 4 guests comfortably,
and measures at 605 square feet, making it the most spacious accommodation available in the entire resort.
Each chalet boasts a spa-inspired bathroom with separate walk-in shower and an extravagant bathtub for extra indulgence.
The exquisite infusion of tasteful traditional elements and architecture in the chalet’s interior and decor, with its tumble stone and tropical wood floorings, sets the perfect ambience for a purely relaxing vacation.
